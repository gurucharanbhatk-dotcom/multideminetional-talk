Host & Advocate

Krupa

Legal Professional & Founder, MultiDimensionalTalks

Krupa is a practising legal professional who created MultiDimensionalTalks to bring the same rigour she applies in a courtroom to public conversation — asking the questions that matter, in plain language, without losing the nuance.

Each episode brings a minister, legislator or public figure into direct, on-record conversation: what a policy actually does, who it affects, and how power is being used. The aim isn't confrontation for its own sake — it's clarity, delivered with the discipline of someone trained to build a case.

The Mission

Why MultiDimensionalTalks exists.

Public figures are easy to see and hard to hear from directly. MultiDimensionalTalks closes that gap — sitting ministers, legislators, and other public figures across the table for conversations that go past the soundbite.

The Approach

Prepared. Direct. Fair.

Every guest is researched with the same care that goes into a legal brief. Questions are direct but fair, giving guests real room to answer — and viewers a clearer picture of the people making decisions on their behalf.
