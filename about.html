Host & Legal Professional

Shree

Legal Professional & Founder, MultiDimensionalTalks

Shree is a practising legal professional with a sharp command of policy, law and governance — the kind of grounding that lets her ask a minister about a bill, a CA about a loophole, or a doctor about a health policy, and genuinely understand the answer. That command of the system is what makes every MultiDimensionalTalks conversation land with substance instead of small talk.

But long before she sat across from ministers and public figures, Shree built her own path the hard way — through years of rigorous legal study, real cases, and the quiet discipline it takes to master a field that rewards persistence far more than shortcuts. That journey taught her something she now carries into every episode: real credibility isn't inherited, it's earned — and the people who earn it have stories worth telling.

MultiDimensionalTalks is Shree living out that belief. It's her answer to a simple question — if her own journey from student to legal professional could inspire even one person, what could a platform built to amplify dozens of such journeys do for an entire country? Every episode is built to do two things at once: reveal the human being behind the title, and make sense of the moments that shake society — so that people don't just consume the news, they understand it, and are inspired enough to do something with that understanding.

What Shree Stands For

Three beliefs behind every episode.

01

Knowledge is a responsibility.

Understanding law, policy and public systems isn't a private advantage — it's something worth putting back into the world, one honest conversation at a time.

02

Every journey can inspire another.

Shree's own path from student to legal professional is proof that discipline changes lives. She built MultiDimensionalTalks so that proof isn't hers alone — it's something every guest, and every viewer, can draw strength from.

03

Society changes through understanding.

People don't act on headlines they don't understand. Every episode is built to leave you clearer — about a person, an issue, or both — so you're equipped to think and act for yourself.

The Mission

Why MultiDimensionalTalks exists.

Public figures are easy to see and hard to hear from directly. MultiDimensionalTalks closes that gap — sitting ministers, senior advocates, CAs, doctors, business leaders, the Lokayukta and other public figures across the table for conversations that go past the soundbite, and turning the country's biggest incidents into stories people can actually understand.

The Approach

Prepared. Direct. Fair.

Every guest is researched with the same care that goes into a legal brief. Questions are direct but fair, giving guests real room to answer — and viewers a clearer picture of the people making decisions on their behalf, and the moments shaping the country they live in.
