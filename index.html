Two Ways We Look

The person, and the moment.

MultiDimensionalTalks runs on two ideas — who's really behind the position, and what's really behind the headline.

Pillar One

Know the person.

Ministers, senior advocates, CAs, doctors, business leaders, the Lokayukta and other public figures — before the title, they're people with a story. We sit down to understand what shaped them, what drives them, and how their work quietly moves society forward.

  • Ministers
  • Senior Advocates
  • CAs
  • Doctors
  • Business Leaders
  • Lokayukta
Pillar Two

Understand the issue.

When something big breaks — an incident, a controversy, a policy fallout — the headlines move fast and the facts get thin. We slow down to lay out what actually happened, why it happened, and who is involved, so you understand the issue, not just the outrage.
